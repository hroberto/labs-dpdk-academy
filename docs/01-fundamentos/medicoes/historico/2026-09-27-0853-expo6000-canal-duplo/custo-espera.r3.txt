  The primitive is not the problem: an uncontended mutex costs a few ns.
  The SAME mutex costs 97 ns without sleeping and 1350 ns with a condvar --
  14x of difference, and the only variable changed was sleeping or not.

  Budget of a 64 B packet at 10 GbE: 67.2 ns
    busy waiting fits 3.6 times in it
    sleeping spends 20.1 whole budgets

  That is why the data plane polls: there is no time to sleep.
  The price is occupying 100% of the core even with no traffic at all.
