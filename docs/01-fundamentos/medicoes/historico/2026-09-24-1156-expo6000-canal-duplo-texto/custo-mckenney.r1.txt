  For comparison, McKenney's values in CYCLES (0.6 ns period):
    CAS best case    63 | lock best case   109
    cache miss      232 | CAS with miss     510

  McKenney's system has 4 sockets; there 'another core' can
  mean another socket. Here the equivalent distinction is between the
  two L3 domains -- the last two rows against the two
  before them. That is the SOSP 2013 conclusion: the scalability of
  synchronisation is, above all, a property of the hardware.
