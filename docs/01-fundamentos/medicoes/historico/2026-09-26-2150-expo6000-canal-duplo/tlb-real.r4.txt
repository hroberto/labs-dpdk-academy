Real TLB of this machine, read from CPUID

  page               L1 DTLB   L2 DTLB   L2 reach
  ----------------   -------   -------   --------
  4 KB                    96      4096           16 MB
  2 MB (hugepage)         96      4096            8 GB
  1 GB (hugepage)         96      1024         1024 GB

  L2 multiplier (L2TlbSizeX32): 32x
  RAW CPUID values: 128 (4 KB) and 128 (2 MB)

  The kernel publishes the raw values up to version 7.4. Check:
    grep -m1 'TLB size' /proc/cpuinfo
  If the number there does not match the L2 column above, this is why.

  Note the 1 GB row: a SEPARATE structure, 4-way, 1024 entries.
  The formula 'reach = entries x page' assumes the number of
  entries does not change with page size -- a premise that holds
  from 4 KB to 2 MB and BREAKS at 1 GB -- by a factor of 4,
  not by the 512x the page size alone would suggest.
