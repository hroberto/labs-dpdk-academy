  Sharing the core costs 175% of performance; using distinct
  physical cores costs 1%. Two logical CPUs are not two
  cores: in a polling loop, which never yields the execution
  units, the SMT sibling competes all the time.

  The 2.73x degradation of the observed thread does NOT predict this:

  Crossing the interconnect costs 4.1x more.
