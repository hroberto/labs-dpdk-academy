  Sharing the core costs 130% of performance; using distinct
  physical cores costs 1%. Two logical CPUs are not two
  cores: in a polling loop, which never yields the execution
  units, the SMT sibling competes all the time.

  The 2.29x degradation of the observed thread does NOT predict this:

  Crossing the interconnect costs 3.7x more.
