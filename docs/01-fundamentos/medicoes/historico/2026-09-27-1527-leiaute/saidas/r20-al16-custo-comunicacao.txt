  Sharing the core costs 175% of performance; using distinct
  physical cores costs 2%. Two logical CPUs are not two
  cores: in a polling loop, which never yields the execution
  units, the SMT sibling competes all the time.

  The 2.74x degradation of the observed thread does NOT predict this:
  applied symmetrically, it would give 0.73x.

  Crossing the interconnect costs 4.3x more.
  That is 121% of the 67.2 ns budget of a 64 B packet at 10 GbE:
  a single handoff between badly placed cores already blows it.
