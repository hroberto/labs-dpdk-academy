  The first two columns are AMORTISED time; the third is
  LATENCY. Sequential costs nearly the same at any size,
  because the prefetcher hides the latency. Random degrades, but
  still amortises: the addresses come from a vector read in order,
  so several accesses happen at once. Only the dependent
  chain exposes the full price of ONE access.

  The ratio between the second and third columns is how many accesses
  the machine keeps in flight. See custo-paralelismo.c.
